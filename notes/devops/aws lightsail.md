##### Content
A simpler alternative to EC2, RDS, ELB, EBS, Route 53, etc
- Provides virtual servers, storage, DB, and networking
- Offers low and predictable pricing
- Ideal for individuals with limited cloud experience
- Allows setup of notifications and monitoring for its resources
- Offers high availability
- Lacks auto-scaling and has limited AWS integrations

### Use Cases
- Simple web apps with templates (LAMP, Nginx etc)
- Development and testing envs
