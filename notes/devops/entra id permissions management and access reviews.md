### Advanced Identity Governance
Managing the lifecycle of identities and permissions.

### Permissions Management
- Granular control across multi-cloud environments (Azure, AWS, GCP).
- Analyzes permissions to suggest removals for unused high-privilege access.

### Access Reviews
- Periodic automated checks to verify if users still need their assigned access.
- Can be self-reviewed by users or reviewed by designated managers.
