### Governance Fundamentals
Governance ensures that resources are provisioned and managed according to corporate standards. In the cloud, where users provision resources directly, automated enforcement is required.

### Governance Framework
- On-prem: Governance is enforced by human gatekeepers (Ops teams).
- Cloud: Governance is enforced by automated guardrails (Policies and RBAC) sitting on top of the Azure Resource Manager (ARM) layer.
- Microsoft Purview: Used to track compliance and data governance across the entire estate.
