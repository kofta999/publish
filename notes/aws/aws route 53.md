##### Content
A DNS service, managed by routing policies

Some policies include:
- Simple routing policy
- Weighted: Distributes traffic across multiple resources based on assigned weights 
- Latency: Routes traffic to the resource with the lowest latency
- Failover: Routes traffic to a standby resource in case of failures

Does not do any health checks
