##### Content
It's a model referring to **protection of your cloud computing resources**, whose responsibility is shared between the customer and AWS

- AWS is responsible for
	- Security of the Cloud: Protecting infra
	- Includes **physical security, environmental controls, and maintaining core infra services**

- The Customer is responsible for
	- Security in the cloud: Protecting their apps, data and identity management
	- Includes **securing data, managing access controls, and configuring security settings** for individual services
