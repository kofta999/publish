### Conditional Access
The "If-Then" engine for Azure security.

### Logic Flow
- Signals: Who is the user? What is their location? What device are they on? What app are they hitting?
- Decision: Block access, Grant access, or Grant with requirements (MFA).
- **Requirement:** Entra ID P1 or higher is required.
